Cardano4All Pay · Escrow-style plan — trusted arbiter · By @kshot9000

An escrow-style ADA plan — with a trusted arbiter, not a contract.

For buying from someone you do not know yet: buyer, seller and a mutually trusted arbiter agree a release condition in writing; the buyer funds the arbiter with the full item amount plus the disclosed 1% service fee, and on release the arbiter pays the seller in full — or refunds the buyer in full. The fee is earned at funding and is never deducted from either second-step payment.

Not a smart-contract escrow — read this first

A plain Cardano payment cannot hold funds in escrow — that takes a smart contract — so this is not a smart-contract escrow: the page holds nothing, and nothing is locked on chain. The plan text leads with that warning so it cannot be trimmed off when the plan is forwarded. Only use an arbiter all three parties trust, and this page builds no transaction and signs nothing.
