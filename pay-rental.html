Plan rent + deposit in ADA — fee in the open, deposit in full.

The upfront rental payment, planned in writing: the first rent and the refundable security deposit, both in full to the landlord's mainnet payment address, plus the same disclosed 1% service fee on the upfront total (rent + deposit). The fee is paid on top and is never part of the deposit — the plan states the landlord refunds the full deposit, subject to the written lease. A deposit of 0 ADA is allowed and is stated as none.

Not a lease — read this first

This is not a lease or rental agreement: it creates no tenancy, the page holds no deposit, and nothing is locked on chain. Whether the deposit comes back is set by the written lease and local law — the plan text leads with that warning. This page builds no transaction and signs nothing.

The fee, in full

It is calculated on the item amount — for a rental plan, on the upfront total of rent plus deposit; for a retainer plan, on each monthly payment; for a milestone plan, on each stage payment; for a group plan, on each payer's share — floored to whole lovelace with exact integer maths, and it is always an extra output the buyer pays — never deducted from what a seller receives.
