Cardano4All Pay · Installment plan — one price paid in parts · By @kshot9000

Pay one price in equal parts in ADA — fee in the open.

The installment shape, written as a plan: one total price paid by one buyer in 2 to 12 equal parts over time — a rig, a camera, a domain. Every part but the last is the floored equal part and the last takes the remainder, so the parts always add up to the price exactly, and each part payment carries the same disclosed 1% service fee on that part — a part that is never paid was never charged a fee. The shareable link carries no fee field, so it cannot be edited to raise or reroute the fee.

Not credit or financing — read this first

This is not credit, a loan or financing: the page does not lend anything, does not check or affect anyone's credit, cannot enforce a part payment, charge interest or late fees, or repossess anything — each part is a separate manual payment in the buyer's own wallet, and the seller should not hand over the item until the final part is paid unless they choose to in writing. This page builds no transaction and signs nothing.

It is calculated on the item amount — for a rental plan, on the upfront total of rent plus deposit; for a retainer plan, on each monthly payment; for a milestone plan, on each stage payment; for a group plan, on each payer's share; for an installment plan, on each part payment — floored to whole lovelace with exact integer maths, and it is always an extra output the buyer pays — never deducted from what a seller receives.
