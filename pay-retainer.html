Cardano4All Pay · Retainer plan — recurring monthly payments · By @kshot9000

A retainer plan in ADA — recurring payments, fee in the open.

The recurring shape, written as a plan: the same monthly amount paid each month for an agreed number of months (1–24), a note of the service it covers, and your mainnet payment address, as copyable text or a shareable link to send a client. Each monthly payment is the full monthly amount plus the same disclosed 1% service fee — charged on each monthly payment — and the plan writes out the per-payment fee and the full-term totals, so the fee is no surprise in month seven. The shareable link carries no fee field, so it cannot be edited to raise or reroute the fee.

Not a subscription — read this first

This is not a subscription and nothing here charges automatically: Cardano payments from this plan are manual — one payment per month, made by the client in their own wallet. The page sets up no recurring payment, holds no funds, cannot pull a payment and cannot stop or change the arrangement; the retainer terms are whatever the provider and the client agree in writing. This page builds no transaction and signs nothing.
