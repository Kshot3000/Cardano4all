Cardano4All Pay · Split payment plan — two sellers · By @kshot9000

Split an ADA sale between two sellers — fee in the open.

Splitting a sale with a partner, written as a plan: seller 1 gets the percentage share you enter, seller 2 gets the rest, so the two shares always add up to the item amount exactly — no lovelace lost to rounding. The buyer pays the same disclosed 1% service fee on the total, on top of both shares. The two sellers must be different mainnet payment addresses, each with a verifying checksum.

What this plan is — and is not

This page builds no transaction and signs nothing — the buyer's own wallet makes the payment from the plan text. A share that would floor to 0 lovelace is refused rather than written as a plan that pays one seller nothing, and any output under 1 ADA is named in a minimum-UTxO warning.

The fee, in full
