Cardano4All Pay · Marketplace listing — for sale · By @kshot9000

Write a for-sale listing in ADA — fee in the open.

The listing itself, not just a payment request: an item title, an asking price, your mainnet payment address and a delivery-terms note, written as copyable text or a shareable link to post wherever you sell. The buyer pays the full asking price plus the same disclosed 1% service fee on top. The shareable link carries no fee field, so it cannot be edited to raise or reroute the fee.

Not a marketplace — read this first

This is not a marketplace: the page holds no listings, takes no custody and has not seen or verified the item — the listing is seller-written and says so itself, before the asking price. Buyers are told to agree delivery in writing or use the escrow-style plan instead. This page builds no transaction and signs nothing.
