Cardano4All Pay · Group plan — several payers split one bill · By @kshot9000

Split one bill between several payers in ADA — fee in the open.

The group shape, written as a plan: one total bill split equally between 2 to 10 payers — friends splitting a dinner, a team splitting a cost, a group buy. Every payer but the last pays the floored equal share and the last takes the remainder, so the shares always add up to the bill exactly, and each payer's payment carries the same disclosed 1% service fee on that payer's share. The shareable link carries no fee field, so it cannot be edited to raise or reroute the fee.

Not a collection service — read this first

This is not a crowdfunding or collection service: the page does not collect payments, does not track who has paid and cannot chase a payer who does not pay — each payer pays separately in their own wallet, and the seller keeps their own record of which payers have paid. Agree what the bill is for in writing before anyone pays. This page builds no transaction and signs nothing.

The fee, in full

The 1% service fee is the whole business model of these tools, and it is printed in every plan next to the address it goes to — my Cardano4All fee address, the same ADA address as in Support below:

It is calculated on the item amount — for a rental plan, on the upfront total of rent plus deposit; for a retainer plan, on each monthly payment; for a milestone plan, on each stage payment; for a group plan, on each payer's share — floored to whole lovelace with exact integer maths, and it is always an extra output the buyer pays — never deducted from what a seller receives.
