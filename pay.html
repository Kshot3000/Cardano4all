Get paid in ADA — with the fee in the open.

Six payment-plan builders for paying in ADA. Each one writes the payment as plain copyable text — who gets what, in full — and each carries the same disclosed 1% service fee: 1% of the item amount, floored to whole lovelace, paid by the buyer on top to my Cardano4All fee address and printed in the plan itself. No hidden cut, nothing taken out of the seller's amount. The pages build no transaction and sign nothing — the buyer's own wallet makes the payment.

The six plans

Pick by how the payment is shaped. All six check every address is a real mainnet payment address with a verifying checksum before a plan is written, and all five can copy a shareable link that opens the builder with the details filled in — the link carries no fee field of its own, so it cannot be edited to raise or reroute the fee: the 1% is worked out by the page every time it opens.

  • Payment plan — one seller

    The straightforward sale. The buyer pays the full item amount to your mainnet payment address, plus the disclosed 1% service fee on top. Amounts under 0.01 ADA floor the fee to 0 lovelace, and the plan says so.

    Build a payment plan

  • Split payment plan — two sellers

    Splitting a sale with a partner: seller 1 gets the percentage share you enter, seller 2 gets the rest, so the two shares always add up to the item amount exactly — no lovelace lost to rounding. The buyer pays the same disclosed 1% fee on the total, on top of both shares. The two sellers must be different addresses.

    Build a split payment plan

  • Escrow-style plan — trusted arbiter

    For buying from someone you do not know yet. Be clear about what this is: a plain Cardano payment cannot hold funds in escrow — that takes a smart contract — so this is not a smart-contract escrow, the page holds nothing, and nothing is locked on chain. Instead, buyer, seller and a mutually trusted arbiter agree a release condition in writing; the buyer funds the arbiter with the full item amount plus the disclosed 1% fee, and on release the arbiter pays the seller in full — or refunds the buyer in full. Only use an arbiter all three parties trust.

    Build an escrow-style plan

  • Marketplace listing — for sale

    The listing itself, not just a payment request: an item title, an asking price, your payment address and delivery terms, written as copyable text (or a shareable link) to post wherever you sell. The buyer pays the full asking price plus the same disclosed 1% service fee. Be clear about the boundary: this is not a marketplace — the page holds no listings and has not seen or verified the item; the listing says so itself.

    Build a marketplace listing

  • Rental deposit plan — rent + deposit

    The upfront rental payment, planned in writing: the first rent and the refundable security deposit, both in full to the landlord, plus the same disclosed 1% service fee on the upfront total (rent + deposit). The fee is paid on top and is never part of the deposit — the plan states the landlord refunds the full deposit, subject to the written lease. Be clear about the boundary: this is not a lease — the page holds no deposit, nothing is locked on chain, and whether the deposit comes back is set by the written lease and local law.

    Build a rental deposit plan

  • Invoice plan — freelance & service work

    The invoice itself, not just a payment request: an invoice number, a note of the work done, the amount and your payment address, written as copyable text (or a shareable link) to send a client. The client pays the full invoiced amount plus the same disclosed 1% service fee. Be clear about the boundary: this is not an accounting or tax system — the page holds no records and has not verified the work; the invoice says so itself, and both parties keep their own records for tax.

    Build an invoice plan

How a plan is used

  • 1. Build the plan. Enter the address(es), the item amount and an optional label; the builder checks the addresses and writes the exact outputs as text.
  • 2. Send it unchanged. Copy the plan text, or copy the shareable link and send that — the link refills the builder and rebuilds the same plan, fee included, on the other side.
  • 3. The buyer pays in their own wallet. They paste the outputs into their wallet, double-check every address in full, and pay the item amount, the fee, and the network fee their wallet adds. Nothing on these pages ever touches a wallet or a key.
